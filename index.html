pane

Your Mac, with a taskbar.

pane brings the way Windows handles windows to macOS: a taskbar in place of the Dock, a Start menu on a tap of ⌘, snapping, Alt+Tab between windows, and apps that quit when their last window closes.

What pane adds to your Mac

Everything you reach for on a PC, working with the Mac apps you already use. Your macOS shortcuts keep working too.

  • A taskbar in place of the Dock

    One button per window or per app, pinned apps, badges, and a clock with its calendar. Show desktop sits at the far corner. When pane quits, your Dock comes back as it was.

  • Start, on a tap of ⌘

    Search your apps, keep favourites in Pinned, browse All apps as a grid or a list, and lock, sleep, restart or shut down from the same place.

  • Snap to edges and corners

    Drag a window to an edge or a corner and see where it will land before you let go. Double-click a title bar or press the green button to maximize.

  • Alt+Tab between windows

    Press ⌥Tab for every open window, most recent first, so two windows of the same app are two stops instead of one.

  • Close the last window, quit the app

    As on Windows, an app quits when its last window closes, so nothing stays running out of sight. Add apps to the opt-out list to keep them open.

  • A taskbar on every display

    Maximize and snaps fit each display. Windows keep their snap when they move between displays, and unplugging one brings its windows home.

pane changes a few settings, and always puts them back

pane changes as little as it can, keeps a copy of anything it does change, and works without turning off any of your Mac's protections.

One permission
pane needs Accessibility, and nothing else, to run. Screen Recording and file access are optional: without them, only the feature that uses them is turned off.
Your settings, restored
pane saves the Dock and tiling settings it changes and restores them when it quits, when you log out, or the next time it starts after a crash.
No changes to System Integrity Protection
pane uses a few private macOS APIs where public ones can't do the job, such as focusing one window of an app. SIP stays on.
Open source at its core
The engine is on GitHub with its tests. Read the code, or build it yourself.

Questions

Which Macs does pane run on?

Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. pane is designed for macOS 26, where the taskbar uses the same glass as the Dock and the menu bar.

What happens to the Dock?

The taskbar takes its place. pane shrinks the Dock and tucks it under the taskbar while it runs, and puts it back as it was when pane quits.

Will my Mac shortcuts still work?

Yes. pane's own shortcuts use keys macOS leaves free, like ⌃⌥ and an arrow. If you'd rather use the Windows layout, pane Pro has a Windows keyboard you can turn on in Settings.

I already use another switcher or menu-bar app. Will they clash?

When pane first starts, it looks for apps that do the same jobs, such as other Alt+Tab switchers and menu-bar organisers, and lets you choose which one does what.

Why isn't pane on the Mac App Store?

The App Store doesn't accept apps that control other apps' windows the way pane does, or that use private macOS APIs. pane is signed and comes straight from its developer.

What happens when the trial ends?

pane switches to Free and keeps running. Pro features turn off, and your Pro settings are kept for when you buy Pro.

Is pane made by Microsoft?

No. pane is an independent app. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Microsoft or Apple. "Windows" describes the way of working that pane brings to the Mac.
